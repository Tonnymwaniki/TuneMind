Role: Senior Industrial & Mobile UI/UX Designer specializing in high-end acoustic hardware interfaces (Teenage Engineering, Braun, Teenage Engineering OP-1, Teenage Engineering OB-4, SSL consoles).
Project: TuneMind — Mobile Personal Music Intelligence App
Viewport: 390 x 844 px (Mobile-first, iPhone 15 Pro / Pixel 8 form factor)
Design Philosophy:
- "Precision Studio Hardware meets Ambient Intelligence."

- Materials: Dark anodized aluminum (#0C1220 to #142235), matte PBT tactile switches, recessed display bezels (#1C2940), knurled metallic sliders, and soft atmospheric backlighting.
- Typography:
  * Primary: SF Pro Display / Plus Jakarta Sans (Clean, elegant, confident)
  * Data/Readouts: JetBrains Mono / Space Mono (Precision studio meters, frequencies, timestamps)
